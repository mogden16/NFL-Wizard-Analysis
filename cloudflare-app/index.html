What do these terms mean?

Negative Binomial is a way to estimate counts, such as catches or carries, while allowing more game-to-game variation than a simpler count method. It helps avoid treating every game as equally predictable.

Model chance of Over is this model's estimated chance the player finishes above the displayed line. Market-implied chance of Over comes from sportsbook Over and Under prices after removing the books' built-in margin. The gap is model chance minus market chance, in percentage points. It is not a return estimate.

Model lean follows the scanner's existing rule: less than five percentage points apart is “Aligned”; otherwise it points to Over or Under. It does not mean that side is more likely than 50%, and it does not override data-quality flags.

How is the ATD price gap measured?

The scan converts each book's price to its implied chance. “Price gap” is the middle market price's implied chance minus the best price's implied chance, in percentage points. A positive gap means one book offered a better payout than the middle book price. It does not use the football model.

The middle price needs at least three sportsbooks. “Price outlier” means the best payout is unusually far from the middle and may need extra checking. “Stale” means a contributing quote was over 15 minutes old at scan time. Quote age is measured at the scan, so an older saved slate must not be treated as live.
